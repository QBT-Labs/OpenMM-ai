Most AI agents today just chat. You ask a question, they generate text. That's it.

Skills change this.

Skills are pre-built capabilities that let AI agents interact with real tools, APIs, and services. Think of them like apps for your AI assistant — except instead of tapping icons, your agent reads a skill file and knows exactly what to do.

Here's the difference:

Without skills → "What's my portfolio balance?"
Agent: "I don't have access to your exchange."

With skills → "What's my portfolio balance?"
Agent: checks MEXC, Gate.io, Bitget, and Kraken → returns your balances in seconds.

No code. No integration work. Just conversation.

The Agent Skills standard makes this portable. One SKILL.md file works across 40+ agents — Codex, Gemini CLI, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code, and many more.
